I mostly use my [web stats service](https://tinylytics.app/) because I get a lizard-brain kick from watching numbers go up, but it also provides some fun insights. For example, it's how I found out about [bubbles.town](https://bubbles.town/).

At first I thought this might be a Mastodon instance. I get a lot of referral traffic from Mastodon, but each instance has its own URL. You can guess at `mas.to`, but `dice.camp` doesn't provide many clues.

Bubbles, [as it's known](https://bubbles.town/about), is a very nice service. It aggregates a curated list of indie blogs. People can sign up and vote for posts they enjoy, but they can't submit new articles. Authentication and comments are both handled using [Fediverse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fediverse) technologies. In my case, I was able to log in via my Mastodon instance.

The idea is to highlight cool indie writers around the web. I love this sort of thing so very much.

Anyway, this is all to say that [you should check out Bubbles](https://bubbles.town/). Seeing a [blog-based reaction](https://onlinegoddess.net/2026/07/re-an-infuriating-goodbye-to-photoshop/) to [one of my posts on the service](https://bubbles.town/entry/44747869) filled my heart with immense joy.
